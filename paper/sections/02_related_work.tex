\section{Related Work}

\citet{petrov2023language} quantify cross-language tokenization inequality.
\citet{ahia2023all} discuss multilingual disparities in model behavior.
MEGA reports elevated fertility for Dravidian languages including Malayalam
and Tamil \citep{mega2024}. The Token Tax framework correlates fertility
with AfriMMLU accuracy \citep{tokentax2024}. IndicSuperTokenizer explores
tokenizer design for Indic languages \citep{indicsupertokenizer2024}.

Our work extends these lines to Telugu with register-aware corpora,
script-fairness pairing, and morphologically labeled minimal pairs.
